About

Bio

I'm a programmer currently working as technical director for Altodata on various projects for the rail industry, using AWS technologies mostly (Lambda, CDK, RDS (PostgreSQL), SNS, SQS etc.), golang and Python.

Before that I worked for OCamlLabs/Tarides on various OCaml and blockchain projects. I have a special interest in filesystems and storage layers.

Prior to this I was a Senior Lecturer at the University of Leicester from 2009 to 2021. My background is in formal methods and theorem proving. My research mainly focused on applications of formal methods to real-world systems, including verified parsing and a verified file system.

At Cambridge I worked on the NetSem project, giving a formal semantics to TCP/IP, and briefly on relaxed memory models.

    I work as technical director, see above.
